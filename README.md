# Super Store Sales & 15 Days Sales Forecast Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Super Store sales data and building an interactive dashboard to track sales performance, profit, quantity, and business trends. It also includes a 15-day sales forecasting dashboard to predict future sales trends.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Understand sales trends across different regions and states.
- Compare sales by customer segments and payment modes.
- Identify top-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Analyze shipping modes and delivery performance.
- Forecast sales for the next 15 days.

## Dashboard 1: Super Store Sales Dashboard

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
- Total Sales: 1.6M
- Total Quantity: 22K
- Total Profit: 175.26K
- Average Delivery: 9.57

### Visualizations:
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Segment
- Sales by Payment Mode
- Sales by Month
- Profit by Month
- Sales by Ship Mode
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Sub-Category
- Sales and Profit by State (Map)

##  Dashboard 2: 15 Days Sales Forecast

### Key Features:
- 15-day future sales forecasting.
- Historical sales trend analysis.
- Actual vs. Forecasted Sales visualization.
- Sales trend analysis over time.

## Tools & Technologies

- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

##  Key Insights
- Analyzed sales performance across different regions and states.
- Identified top-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Compared sales and profit trends over different months.
